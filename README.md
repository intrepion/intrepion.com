# intrepion.com

Intrepion is being explored as an AI-assisted life roadmap system.

The product idea is to help one person manage important life areas through a shared structure:

- six-horizon roadmaps
- supporting records
- recurring reviews
- AI-assisted next actions

Initial life areas include ambitions, career and jobs, people and relationships, food and places, language learning, fitness and routines, travel planning, entertainment, shopping and receipts, and finances and debt.

The first private prototype area is Ambitions, focused on getting oliverforral.com and karinnaaf.art planned, published, and reviewed through the six-horizon model. The public homepage may still use Career as the marketing specimen because it is easier for new visitors to understand quickly.

Prototype artifacts:

- `docs/ambitions-prototype.md`: first Ambitions model, examples, and build boundary.
- `ambitions.html`: static app-shaped mock for the first Ambitions screen.
- `ambition-form.html`: static intake form mock for manually filling in one ambition.

This repository is currently a static marketing and product-spec surface. It intentionally does not implement authentication, billing, banking imports, receipt ingestion, job applications, or external automation yet.
