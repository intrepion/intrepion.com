One rhythm for every life area.

Each area starts with the same calm structure: name what matters, collect the facts, map the next horizons, and review what changed with AI assistance.

1. Area

Name the loop

Start with the thing that keeps resurfacing: debt, job search, language learning, receipts, media, or a project.

2. Records

Capture the facts

Add the bills, applications, receipts, ratings, notes, practice history, or project context the roadmap should use.

3. Roadmap

Split the horizons

Turn the area into 1 hour, 1 day, 1 week, 1 month, 1 year, and 1 decade actions.

4. Review

Let AI help carefully

Summarize what changed, expose missing facts, and suggest next actions without pretending uncertainty is certainty.
