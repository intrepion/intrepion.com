A roadmap should change as the project becomes real.

Long-term architecture matters, but the next useful move matters more. Intrepion keeps both visible.

Six fixed horizons

Plan across 1 hour, 1 day, 1 week, 1 month, 1 year, and 1 decade without pretending every decision belongs in the same bucket.

Tutorial-aware planning

Roadmaps can point users to tutorials for the choices they make, or mark missing tutorials as planned and requestable.

Automation later

Services will eventually help users perform tutorial steps through APIs, with explicit credential control and admin-reviewed workflows.
