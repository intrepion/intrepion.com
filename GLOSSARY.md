# Everyday lists and households

The application helps people manage personal tasks, shopping needs, reusable routines, and shared household lists.

## Language

**Task**:
An item of work in a personal or household space, with a title, optional notes, and a state of complete or incomplete.
_Avoid_: Todo, to-do item

**Shopping list**:
A named collection of items to purchase, reused across shopping trips. Each item can be marked purchased or needed again.

**Checklist template**:
A reusable set of checklist items used to create independent checklist runs. Changes to a template apply to future runs.

**Section**:
A named group of items within one shopping list, checklist template, or checklist run.

**Category**:
A named group of whole lists or checklist templates.

**Checklist run**:
An individual use of a checklist template with its own items and completion progress. Later template changes do not change an existing run.

**Trash**:
A holding place for removed items and collections that are hidden from ordinary views but can be restored. Trashed content remains until explicitly erased.

**Archive**:
A state for a retained shopping list, checklist template, or checklist run that is hidden from active views but remains viewable and can be made active again.

**History**:
A record of successful changes to a task, list, template, or run, including the actor, time, and previous and new values. Collection history includes changes to its items and sections.

**Permanent erasure**:
The irreversible removal of retained content and its associated content history, triggered by explicit confirmation or a previously accepted erasure policy.

**Household**:
A group of members who share lists, routines, and household resources.

**Household closure**:
The end of a household's active participation when its last head leaves, with retained content awaiting scheduled permanent erasure.

**Household member**:
A person belonging to a household.

**Head of household**:
A household member authorized to administer the household, including setting member allowances.

**Member allowance**:
The maximum usage an individual household member is permitted within that household, subject to the household's total capacity.

**Personal space**:
A person's private lists and routines, separate from shared household content.

**Household space**:
A household's shared lists and routines.

**Storage allowance**:
The maximum amount of retained content and History permitted in a personal or household space.

**Subscription**:
A user's plan providing capacity that they may allocate to households.

**Capacity allocation**:
A reserved portion of a user's subscription-provided capacity assigned to one household and unavailable for use elsewhere while allocated.
_Avoid_: Funds, when referring to capacity rather than money
