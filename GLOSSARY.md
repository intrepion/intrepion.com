# intrepion.com

A cozy social game where friends spend time at their homes and play games together.

## Language

**House**:
An in-world home for a player, which may also be shared with friends.

**Land**:
A player's outdoor space in the cozy game.

**Game night**:
A gathering at a player's house where friends play games together.

**Friendship**:
A mutual relationship between two individual profiles, established through an accepted friend invitation and subject to applicable family approval policies. It is separate from family membership and does not itself grant access to private family information.

**Friend invitation**:
A request to establish a friendship between two individual profiles, which a Family Member can initiate. A recipient Family Member can accept it themselves when all their families allow no-admin approval, and can always decline the friendship even if an admin has accepted or approved it.

**Friend circle**:
A private grouping a profile creates to organize its own friends, allowing the same friend in more than one circle. Circle membership does not itself grant access to a house or conversation.

**Profile**:
An individual's identity in the game, separate from the Family Accounts they belong to.

**Family Account**:
A shared family container grouping individual profiles through family membership.

**Family membership**:
A profile's relationship with a Family Account, with the same profile able to belong to multiple Family Accounts under separate roles. Joining a second family requires applicable approval from both families, and the member can decline to join.

**Family Admin**:
An individual profile acting as an administrator within a particular Family Account, which can have multiple admins. The family creator is its first Family Admin and can invite additional admins.

**Family Member**:
An individual profile with the member role in a particular Family Account, subject to that family's policy.

**Family Policy**:
The configurable rules a Family Account's admins choose for their family, including friendship approval requirements and approval for creating member profiles.

**Family approval policy**:
The part of a Family Policy specifying the Family Admin approvals needed for a Family Member's friendship: all admins by default, one admin, or no admins. When a member belongs to multiple families, every applicable family's policy must be satisfied.

**Inactive friendship**:
An existing friendship whose required Family Admin approvals are no longer satisfied. It is retained with its history rather than erased, and can become active again when the applicable family approval policies are satisfied, without overriding a member's decision to decline.

**Family device authorization**:
A Family Account's revocable permission for a particular member to use a device. The same device may have separate authorizations from multiple Family Accounts, each revocable without removing the others.

**Family context**:
The single Family Account selected for a device session. Its authorization does not grant access to another family's private spaces or information, or approve actions governed by that other family's policy.

**Family Admin invitation**:
An invitation for an individual to join a Family Account as a Family Admin, requiring both existing-admin approval and acceptance by the invitee.

**Family invitation**:
An invitation for an individual to join a Family Account. An invited adult must explicitly accept before joining.
