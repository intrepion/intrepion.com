# Normal accounts use independent safety budgets and emailed household invitations

MVP 3.1 replaces the pilot’s operator admission, reserved user contributions and individual head ceilings with invitation-only normal accounts and separate hidden personal/household safety budgets, reducing household setup to its name while preserving retained content, History, attribution and recorded allocations. Google identity plus possession of the recipient-only emailed invitation prepares onboarding; explicit, retry-safe acceptance creates the account and equal-head membership together, without copying private personal data or merging identities by email. Site-wide policy belongs to the configured owner and is reviewed through metadata rather than private content; manual growth pause, bounded SES sending and independently protected revocations preserve the accepted privacy, spending and recovery boundaries while billing/dependent access remain deferred.

This supersedes the active allocation policy in ADR 0005. Migration alone does not enable normal-account safety settings or email onboarding; measured finite settings and provider readiness require their later review gates.

First activation independently records the existing account and head-access baseline before acknowledgment. Later account admissions and access changes use the same journal. Version 4 security events retain their server-allocated unique event IDs through snapshots and publication; older client erasure records retain their existing namespace scheme. Recovery reconciles known public IDs before rebuilding missing profiles, rejects conflicting hash/public-ID mappings, invalidates authentication and pending invitations, and pauses growth. The current UTC month's send counter enters an explicitly labeled conservative recovery hold because a snapshot can omit later attempts.
