# User subscriptions and household capacity are separate from head authority

Users hold subscriptions and may allocate plan-provided capacity to multiple households; the model allows contributions from several heads rather than requiring a household-owned plan or one exclusive sponsor. Administrative authority comes from being a head of household and does not increase with the size of a contribution. Allocations reserve capacity from the contributor's plan, and households add contributions resource by resource; individual member ceilings do not reserve shares of the household pool. This preserves equal head authority across households with different contributors and prevents the same allocated capacity being available in multiple spaces. MVP 3 models capacity without collecting payments; allocations specify independent amounts for each resource. Voluntary reallocations must leave source spaces within their remaining allowances, while genuine plan reductions preserve saved content. Leaving or removal revokes household access but does not automatically release contributed capacity while retained usage depends on it. Last-head departure closes the household permanently and schedules erasure at calendar month-end UTC; allocations remain reserved until erasure succeeds. These retention rules prevent cycling capacity between households while leaving unmetered retained data behind. Detailed policies are recorded in the MVP 3 plan.
