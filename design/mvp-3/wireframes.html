Design gate 1 · wireframes · approved by owner

Everyday lists, together

Review space boundaries, actions and confirmations before visual styling. These static screens extend the approved Everyday lists workflows.

All names, dates, content and numerical allowances are illustrative. Pilot caps remain unapproved. Controls illustrate placement; only the gallery index links navigate. No scripts, services, external assets, authentication or saved data.

Start with 01–08 for household access, 09–14 for capacity, and 16–21 for departure and recovery. Phone layouts are 22–23.
