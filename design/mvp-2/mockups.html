Destructive confirmation

Camping packing · Checklist template

Erases its sections, all items (including individually trashed items), and associated content history.

Existing runs keep their independent copies.

Encrypted backup copies expire under the seven-day retention policy. Recovery reapplies subsequent erasures before reopening the app.

Only available from Trash. Item erasure also removes its content from parent history. Cancel receives the initial focus; failure retains a safe retry.
